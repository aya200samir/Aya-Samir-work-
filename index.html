About the author

A legal perspective informed by applied AI.

Aya Samir is a law graduate of Ain Shams University (English Section), with postgraduate legal study and training in applied AI and data analysis. Her work explores how lawyers can move beyond AI terminology to ask technically meaningful questions, assess evidence, and reason about accountability.

Her AI & Law book is an evolving educational project. The free first chapter demonstrates its case-led approach; future workshops are designed to turn that approach into practical learning exercises.
